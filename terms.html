Service scope

HydroGuard is a HydroLink academic project for monitoring pH and temperature, recording events, and supervising treatment and release according to each device's configuration.

This landing page presents the proposal. Access to the applications requires an account and permissions; visiting this page does not create an account or purchase a service.

Responsible use and safety

Only use accounts and devices you are authorized to manage. Do not share passwords or technical credentials, or attempt to access other organizations' data.

The prototype does not certify drinking-water safety or environmental or health compliance. Decisions about real water require calibrated instruments, independent validation and human supervision.

Do not use the prototype for critical operations or rely on continuous connectivity. On failure, stop the test and physically check the equipment before continuing.

Measurements and actions

Readings depend on calibration, power supply, connections and sensor condition. A displayed reading does not guarantee accuracy or independently authorize release.

Correction and release depend on installed capabilities, applied configuration and system authorizations. A simulated indication is not equivalent to physical dosing.

Availability and external services

The academic environment may experience pauses, restarts and delays. Continuous availability is not offered. Links to applications, images and future videos may depend on external providers.

Data and contact

This landing page's form does not transmit or store inquiries on a server. The language selector saves only your preference in the browser's local storage, when available.

If you use the email link, your email application handles delivery. Do not include passwords, device credentials or sensitive information in inquiries.

Operational data from authenticated applications is not managed through this form. Service-specific terms and policies must be defined before commercial use.
